# Bizi Bees sales and operating plan


Updated October 25, 2026. Store: https://bizibees-store.myshopify.com/


## Priorities


Start with inventory Kimberly already owns. Keep dropshipping alongside resale, with no new monthly supplier subscriptions or paid advertising until orders and margins are verified. No social-media work is planned. Sales and profit are not guaranteed.


## Today


- [ ] Prepare 10 owned items with actual photos, purchase cost, quantity and packed weight.
- [ ] List 5–10 items per day as inventory permits. Include title/ISBN/model, condition, flaws, included parts, measurements, price and shipping weight.
- [ ] Check completed sales for comparable items, not just asking prices; compare shipping-inclusive totals and condition.
- [ ] Track inventory and disable selling when out of stock for every resale variant. Update stock immediately when an item sells elsewhere.
- [ ] Verify the complete order process with one authorized real test order: shipping quote, payment, order confirmation, Kimberly's notification, fulfillment and tracking. A checkout page loading is not a complete transaction test.


## Price for a positive contribution


Per-order contribution = merchandise revenue + shipping collected - item/supplier cost - actual postage/supplier shipping - packaging - payment/platform transaction fees - discounts - refund/return costs.


Exclude collected sales tax from revenue. Subtract monthly overhead separately to measure business profit, and account for labor. Use actual payment fees and costs rather than assumed rates.


The Magnetic Page Marker currently sells for $6 with a $3.60 supplier cost: $2.40 remains BEFORE shipping differences, payment fees and other expenses. This is not $2.40 net profit.


## Book bundle experiment


- [ ] Choose eligible inexpensive books and record their cost.
- [ ] Evaluate an example offer of three selected books for $10 plus shipping; this is a proposal, not an active discount.
- [ ] Check combined packed weight, shipping and payment fees before setting the offer.
- [ ] Limit eligibility to selected Book Deals titles.
- [ ] Test eligible/ineligible items, quantities, sold-out books and checkout totals before advertising.


## Free repeat-customer marketing


- [ ] Include the QR thank-you flyer in packages where the originating marketplace allows it.
- [ ] Scan a printed flyer on a phone and verify the live destination before printing a large batch.
- [ ] Use: “Thank you for shopping from me! Please check out my own store for more goodies like this.”
- [ ] Send weekly new-arrivals emails only to consenting subscribers once newsletter sending and unsubscribe behavior are verified.
- [ ] Ask buyers for honest feedback after delivery, without requiring a positive review.


## Daily operations


Check paid orders, messages and stock. Kimberly ships Bizi Bees-stocked items from Utah by USPS, under the published same-business-day dispatch policy excluding weekends and postal holidays. Use actual packed weights and suitable packaging. Upload tracking and resolve customer issues promptly.
