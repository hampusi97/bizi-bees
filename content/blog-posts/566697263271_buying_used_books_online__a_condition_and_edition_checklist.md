<p>A used book can be a lovely find—provided it is the edition you need and its condition suits the way you will use it. Before you compare prices, check the listing for these five details.</p><h2>1. Match the ISBN and format</h2><p>A familiar cover does not guarantee the same book. Compare the ISBN, author, edition and publication details. Confirm whether the listing is a hardcover, paperback or another format. For a class or book club, use the exact edition requested rather than assuming a similar title will work.</p><p>If a textbook originally included an online access code, do not assume a used copy includes an unused code. Ask before buying if that access matters to you.</p><h2>2. Read the condition notes, not just the grade</h2><p>At Bizi Bees, “like new” means minimal to no visible wear; “good” means visible everyday wear; and “fair” means noticeable wear or cosmetic flaws described in the listing. Our <a href="/pages/condition-guide">condition guide</a> explains the grades. The individual description and photos tell you what to expect from that particular copy.</p><p>Look for notes about highlighting, handwritten names, bent covers, loose pages, a missing dust jacket or other disclosed flaws. A reading copy and a gift copy can have very different requirements. If a detail is missing, ask rather than treating silence as confirmation.</p><h2>3. Check what the photos show</h2><p>Look for the front, back and spine, plus any photographed wear. Check whether the images show the actual copy or are reference images. A stock cover can help identify a title, but it cannot show the wear on a specific used book. You can <a href="/pages/contact">contact Kimberly</a> to ask about a listing before ordering.</p><h2>4. Compare the delivered price</h2><p>Compare the item price plus shipping and applicable tax. A lower book price does not always mean a lower checkout total. If you add several items, review the complete shipping charge before paying; products shipping from different locations may have separate charges.</p><h2>5. Read the return policy before checkout</h2><p>Bizi Bees resale items are final sale for change-of-mind returns. Read the <a href="/policies/refund-policy">refund policy</a> for details and for help with damaged, incorrect or misdescribed items. Supplier-shipped items may have different terms.</p><h2>A quick checklist to keep beside your shopping tab</h2><ul>
<li>Correct title, author, ISBN and edition</li>
<li>The format you actually want</li>
<li>Condition notes and any disclosed writing or damage</li>
<li>Clear information about the copy pictured</li>
<li>Total price, shipping and return terms</li>
</ul><p>Ready to look? <a href="/collections/books">Browse the current Bizi Bees book collection</a>. Availability changes, so use the product page for the latest details. A good find is a book you will enjoy, at a total price you are comfortable paying.</p>
