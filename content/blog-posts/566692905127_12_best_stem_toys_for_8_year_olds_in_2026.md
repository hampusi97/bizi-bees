<p>Age-rated building blocks are the default recommendation for an undecided gift buyer. The activity can start with a suggested structure or with whatever the child wants to make.</p>

<p><strong>Tangram pros:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>The activity has a clear shape-matching goal.</li>
<li>Pieces can also form original pictures.</li>
<li>No power source is needed.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Tangram cons:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Missing pieces affect the puzzles.</li>
<li>Static shapes will not suit every robot enthusiast.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Best for:</strong> A patient puzzler who likes visual challenges.</p>
<p><strong>Verdict: Buy for quiet puzzle time, not for moving mechanisms.</strong></p>
