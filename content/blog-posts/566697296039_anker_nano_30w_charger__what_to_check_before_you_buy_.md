<p>Shopping for a small USB-C wall charger? Start with the device you want to charge, the cable you already own and the number of things you plug in at once. The <a href="/products/anker-nano-charger-30w">Anker Nano Charger (30W)</a> in the Bizi Bees catalog is a compact option to compare—but its wattage alone does not answer every buying question.</p><p>This is a guide based on the product listing, not a hands-on performance test. We have not measured charging speeds or compared this unit against every competing charger.</p><h2>What does the listing include?</h2><p>The supplier identifies this model as the Anker 511 Charger, also called Nano 3, 30W. The listing describes GaN construction and a charger about 1.12 inches thick. It includes the charger and welcome guide; <strong>a cable is not included</strong>. Check the selected variant and current product description before ordering.</p><h2>Check your phone and cable before buying</h2><p>Write down your exact phone or tablet model and check its manufacturer’s charging requirements. Then check both ends of your cable: one must fit the charger and the other must fit your device. Do not assume a USB-C charger means the cable in your drawer will work.</p><p>Bizi Bees also lists <a href="/products/anker-usb-c-to-usb-c-cable-3-ft-6-ft-240w-upcycled-braided">Anker USB-C to USB-C cables</a>. Compare the connector, length and specification with your device’s requirements. A cable’s higher wattage rating does not turn a 30W charger into a higher-output charger.</p><h2>When is this worth considering?</h2><ul>
<li>You want a compact charger and already have a suitable cable.</li>
<li>You are buying for one specific compatible device rather than guessing what someone owns.</li>
