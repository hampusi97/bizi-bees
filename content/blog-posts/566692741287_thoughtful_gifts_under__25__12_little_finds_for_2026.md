<h2>What makes a good small gift?</h2>
<ul>
<li>
<strong>A clear recipient:</strong> A useful gift has someone specific in mind, rather than being a general-purpose purchase.</li>
<li>
<strong>An obvious use:</strong> A page marker marks a page. A quilt pattern needs a maker who wants a project.</li>
<li>
<strong>Enough information:</strong> Condition, contents, dimensions and compatibility should help you decide before you pay.</li>
<li>
<strong>A manageable commitment:</strong> Check whether the gift needs fabric, a cable, other tools or help from an adult.</li>
