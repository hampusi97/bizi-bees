<li>
<strong>Like New (Fine):</strong> The book appears unread. No marks, spine creases, or torn dust jackets.</li>
<li>
<strong>Very Good:</strong> Minimal signs of reading. Spine is intact, pages are clean, light shelf wear.</li>
<li>
<strong>Good:</strong> The standard readable reading copy. May contain light highlighting or spine creasing, but complete text.</li>
<li>
<strong>Acceptable:</strong> Noticeable wear, possible cover creases, but complete and intact.</li>
